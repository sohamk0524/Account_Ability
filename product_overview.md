# Product Interview

Right now, boutique CPA firms waste hours manually parsing client documents and doing menial data entry, or they outsource to international labor just to survive. Existing workarounds are clunky. With rising labor costs and changing tax codes, CPAs lose potential revenue and risk human error. That is where Accountant Ability comes in. We have a working MVP tested with our beachhead customer that targets this exact bottleneck. Instead of spending days digging through files and entering data into legacy software like Lacerte, CPAs simply upload client documents to our platform. Using modern agentic AI tools, our software automatically extracts the relevant info and populates the forms. We completely eliminate the tedious middle steps, letting CPAs jump straight from receiving documents to their final review.

Over the next six months, we are scaling this MVP into a fully deployed platform that handles all IRS documents and natively integrates with existing CPA tools. But our long-term vision goes way beyond tax prep. Within two years, Accountant Ability will be a centralized hub supporting tax planning, portfolio management, and investment planning. Our ultimate goal is to automate the menial busywork across the entire accounting industry. By reducing a CPA’s workload to just high-level supervision, we do more than save them time. We empower boutique firms to drastically scale their capacity, take on more clients, and fundamentally change how they grow their business.
